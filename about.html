My name is Mathias Favre, I'm a Communication Design student at Politecnico di Milano. I'm particularly focused on typography, editorial design, and branding.

Through my studies in the humanities and classics in the Italian Alps, I developed my interest in design around the Greek concept of epiméleia, the practice of attentive care. From this idea comes my belief that design should look beyond form and function to care for people, the environment, culture, and context.

Moving to Milan, I immersed myself in the tradition of Milanese visual design, inspired by masters such as Bruno Munari and Albe Steiner, who brought a sense of elegance, vitality, and experimentation to Swiss structural rigour through colour, texture, and perception.

I believe that design is about creating a landscape: a continuous system with its context, one that people interact with without necessarily noticing, shaped by a gaze that is at once "careful" and "full of care".
